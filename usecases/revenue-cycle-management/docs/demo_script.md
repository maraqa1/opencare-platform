# RCM Demo Script

1. Start on Cash Command and explain the story from care delivered to cash collected.
2. Show revenue at risk and recoverable queue value.
3. Open Recovery Queue and show ranked opportunities by expected recovery and effort.
4. Promote a recovery item into the Decision Queue.
5. Open Decision Queue and show approval, revision, dispatch, escalation, and audit.
6. Open Payer Control to explain underpayment and slow-payment behavior.
7. Open Revenue Leakage to show root causes.
8. Open Team Performance to show owner accountability.
9. Download or open the Board Pack and close with Data Trust.
