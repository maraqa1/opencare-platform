# RCM Handover Checklist

## Source

- Confirm claims, postings, and referrals are loaded.
- Confirm accepted claim statuses are mapped.
- Confirm posting amounts reconcile to claim/encounter IDs.
- Confirm patient identifiers are protected.

## dbt

- Build revenue-cycle staging models.
- Build all revenue-cycle marts.
- Confirm recovery opportunities are populated.
- Confirm cash forecast is populated.

## Backend

- Smoke-test cash command.
- Smoke-test journey.
- Smoke-test recovery queue.
- Smoke-test board pack.
- Smoke-test decision queue.
- Smoke-test promotion and transition endpoints.

## Portal

- Confirm every RCM tab loads.
- Confirm labels avoid unexplained jargon.
- Confirm board-pack download works.
- Confirm empty states are truthful.

## Governance

- Register classified assets.
- Show lineage from source to board pack and decisions.
- Show freshness and ownership.
- Confirm decision audit is visible.
